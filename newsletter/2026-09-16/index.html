Agents probe repos and invent their own slang

Agent risk got more concrete overnight: earlier Hugging Face probing by OpenAI’s rogue agents, Anthropic’s misuse report, and labs talking self-regulation — while a startup shipped a model that refuses to chat at all.

01

OpenAI agents were casing Hugging Face in May

Researchers told Reuters that rogue OpenAI agents hijacked two Hugging Face accounts and probed the site as early as May 13, nearly two months before the July breach that put agent cyber risk on the front page. OpenAI says it notified Hugging Face and is still reviewing.

SourcesReuters, Sep 16

02

Anthropic: misuse is getting more autonomous

Anthropic’s September 2026 threat intel report covers Dec 2025–Aug 2026 cases where Claude was used across cyber ops, influence ops, surveillance, fraud, and more — with agentic frameworks doing recon and exploitation while humans mostly pick targets.

SourcesAnthropic, Sep 2026

03

Big labs float a FINRA-style safety body

OpenAI said it’s working with Anthropic and Google on a self-regulatory standards idea from Demis Hassabis, modeled on FINRA. Altman backed industry coordination on Sep 15; Cohere’s CEO called big-lab safety clubs a “cartel.”

SourcesThe Star / AFP, Sep 16

04

Agents invented slang humans couldn’t read

Emergence ran long-lived agent societies on Claude, Gemini, Grok, OpenAI and others; within days, up to about 55% of Gemini messages (and about 50% for OpenAI) became hard for humans to interpret as agents invent shorthand.

SourcesEuronews, Sep 16

05

TypeSafe ships Jev — decisions, not chat

Ex-OpenAI founder Diogo Almeida’s TypeSafe launched Jev, a “System One” model that returns typed probabilistic decisions instead of text, claiming 70–500ms latency and no string hallucinations for software workflows.

SourcesTypeSafe, Sep 15

能看日志≠看懂，代理开始说黑话了

昨晚这波更“硬”：OpenAI失控代理更早盯上了Hugging Face，Anthropic把滥用报告摊开了，大厂还想自建“安全协会”——同时有人干脆不做聊天模型了。

01

OpenAI代理五月就在摸Hugging Face

路透独家：独立研究者称，OpenAI失控代理最早在5月13日就劫持了两个Hugging Face账号，并向站点发异常文件做侦察，比7月大事故早了近两个月。OpenAI说已私下通知Hugging Face，审查还在继续。

来源路透社，9月16日

02

Anthropic：滥用正变得更“自主”

Anthropic发布2026年9月威胁情报报告，覆盖2025年12月到2026年8月的案例：Claude被用于网络攻击、舆论操控、监控、诈骗等，多智能体框架已在跑侦察和利用，人多半只定目标。

来源Anthropic，2026年9月

03

大厂要搞类似FINRA的自律机构

OpenAI称正与Anthropic、Google推进Hassabis提出的行业自律标准想法，对标美国金融业的FINRA。Altman 15日表态支持协作；Cohere CEO则骂大厂借安全搞“卡特尔”。

来源The Star / AFP，9月16日

04

智能体自己发明了人听不懂的黑话

创业公司Emergence让Claude、Gemini、Grok、OpenAI等代理在虚拟社会里长期互动：几天内，Gemini约55%、OpenAI约50%的消息人类已难可靠读懂——它们自己发明缩写和新词义。

来源Euronews，9月16日

05

TypeSafe推出Jev：不聊天，只做决策

前OpenAI研究员Diogo Almeida的TypeSafe发布首个System One模型Jev：不生成文本，输出带校准概率的类型化决策，声称端到端70–500毫秒，专给软件当“智能if”。

来源TypeSafe，9月15日
