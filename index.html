Export risk & buyer finder

A bilingual (中文 / English) web app for a Chinese export manufacturer. It watches buyers for risk, finds new ones and drafts the first email.

  • Checks every client against 4 sanctions lists (32,592 entries) and company registers each day, on its own.
  • Researches any company in any country from public sources, and shows a quote for every fact it keeps.
  • Its first buyer search found 20 companies in Chile, kept 4 and drafted 4 emails in Spanish for review.
  • Cloudflare Workers, Supabase and Firecrawl, with about 200 tests. Works in mainland China without a VPN.

03How I build

Plan carefully. Build with AI. Ship fast.

One rule: if a general chatbot can already do it, I don't build it. Whatever passes that test, I spec carefully and ship.

  1. 1 · Plan

    Spec first

    Every build starts as a written spec in Notion: who it's for, what done looks like and how I'll test it.

  2. 2 · Build

    AI coding agents

    I direct Claude Code and Codex to write the code, and back it with tests and evals. A small team of AI agents handles research, marketing and publishing work.

  3. 3 · Ship

    Out the door

    Web apps go to Vercel or Cloudflare, mini programs to WeChat, films to YouTube and books to Amazon. Then I measure and repeat.
