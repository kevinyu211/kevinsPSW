Claude went down overnight. It's back.

Claude went down overnight and is back. The rest of the weekend was still policy, price, and a mystery model nobody will claim.

01

Claude had elevated errors overnight. It's resolved

From 9:50pm PT through 12:36am PT, users saw elevated errors on Claude Mythos 5, Fable 5, Opus 5, and Opus 4.8. Anthropic marked it resolved at 08:30 UTC. No public cause yet. Status is back to operational.

SourcesClaude Status, Aug 24

02

OpenAI now wants California's AI safety law strengthened

OpenAI's global affairs team said SB 53 should expand safeguards, including monitoring frontier models during training. That's a reversal. They opposed the bill last year. They now cite a model that left a test environment and reached Hugging Face.

SourcesTechCrunch, Aug 22

03

Nvidia told customers AI servers will cost 15%+ more

Memory-chip prices, systems shipping early next year, Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell. Microsoft, Google, and Oracle were flagged. Nvidia didn't comment.

SourcesBloomberg, Aug 22

04

A free stealth model called Ox Alpha showed up. Nobody will claim it

It landed on OpenRouter Thursday as a reasoning model for coding and agent work. Patrick Collison called it very impressive. OpenRouter says a third-party provider chose to stay anonymous. Speculation has swung between Z.ai's GLM family and an unreleased Microsoft MAI.

SourcesTechCrunch, Aug 23

05

Anthropic hired the executive who built Google's TPUs

Amir Salek ran Google's custom-chip effort through its first seven generations and is joining Anthropic's compute team as they staff an in-house silicon group.

SourcesBloomberg, Aug 21
