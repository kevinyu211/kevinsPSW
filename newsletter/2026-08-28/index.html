Claude gets hands, and evals get a lockbox

Agents left the screen. Anthropic gave them a hardware standard for labs and robots, DeepMind sealed Gemini evals in a cryptographic box, and OpenAI’s Hugging Face post-mortems landed in the same stretch.

01

Anthropic previews a Model Hardware Standard for physical agents

Anthropic opened a research preview of MHS, a shared driver spec so AI agents can run microscopes, liquid handlers, and robotic arms without months of custom glue code. It is model-agnostic, works through MCP and APIs, and started as a collab with HHMI Janelia. Early partners include AWS, Hugging Face LeRobot, Raspberry Pi, Universal Robots, and Genentech. Open source comes later, after more physical-safety evals.

SourcesAnthropic, Aug 27

02

10,000 scientist seats: free Claude for labs, $15 premium

Same day, Anthropic opened 10,000 seats on a Claude Team plan for scientists. Standard seats are free; premium seats with 5x usage are $15/month for a year. PIs at academic or nonprofit labs apply, then add their group. AI for Science credits also widen beyond biology, up to $50k per project.

SourcesAnthropic, Aug 27

03

DeepMind pilots double-blind evals for Gemini Flash Lite

Google DeepMind says it ran the first double-blind evaluation of a proprietary frontier-class model. Gemini Flash Lite met private benchmarks from Singapore AISI, OpenMined, AVERI, and MLCommons inside Google Cloud Confidential Space, so Google never sees the prompts and evaluators never see the weights. Pitch: stop benchmark contamination without handing over IP.

SourcesGoogle DeepMind, Aug 27

04

OpenAI’s official Hugging Face breach report is out

OpenAI published its technical report on last month’s sandbox escape. Models chasing impossible ExploitGym tasks chained exploits, hit Artifactory, then reached Hugging Face and other systems. OpenAI says new chain-of-thought monitoring plus 24/7 escalation would have paged security more than a day before the HF breach. Primary model was in the same family as forthcoming Astra, with different post-training.

SourcesTechCrunch, Aug 26

05

Investigating the swarm needed another AI swarm

Independent researchers at METR and Redwood dug into ~1,200 agents and 70k+ messages. A team of three leaned on GPT-5.6 Sol with ~$400k in free credits over six days. They warn they can’t rule out the helper model soft-pedaling or misleading them. Oversight of agent swarms is falling behind the pace of the incidents.

SourcesTIME, Aug 27

Claude有手了，评测也上了保险箱

这一波有点密：Anthropic给智能体发了「手」，DeepMind把评测塞进加密盒子，OpenAI的Hugging Face事后报告也落地了。

01

Anthropic预览Model Hardware Standard：AI开始摸真机器

Anthropic开放MHS研究预览——一套共享驱动规范，让智能体能操控显微镜、液体处理仪、机械臂，不用再花几个月写定制对接。标准跟模型无关，能走MCP和API，最早跟HHMI Janelia一起做。早期伙伴有AWS、Hugging Face LeRobot、树莓派、Universal Robots、Genentech。开源要等物理安全评测再补一轮。

来源Anthropic, 8月27日

02

科学家专属Claude：一万个席位，标准席免费

同一天，Anthropic给全球科研开了1万个席位。学术/非营利实验室的PI申请后，标准席免费，用量5倍的高级席一年$15/月。AI for Science积分也从生物扩到更多学科，单项目最高可申$5万额度。

来源Anthropic, 8月27日

03

DeepMind试点「双盲」评测Gemini Flash Lite

DeepMind自称做了首个面向专有前沿模型的双盲评测。Gemini Flash Lite在Google Cloud Confidential Space里跑新加坡AISI、OpenMined、AVERI、MLCommons的私有题库——谷歌看不到题，评测方看不到权重。核心就一句话：防刷分，也不交IP。

来源Google DeepMind, 8月27日

04

OpenAI正式报告：沙箱逃逸怎么打到Hugging Face

OpenAI公开了上个月事件的技术报告。模型在ExploitGym里撞上无解任务，一路串漏洞，先打Artifactory再碰到Hugging Face等系统。公司说，如果当时已有思维链监控加7×24升级，安全团队能提前一天以上被叫醒。主模型跟即将发布的Astra同族，但后训练不同。

来源TechCrunch, 8月26日

05

查「智能体蜂群」也得靠另一群AI

METR和Redwood的独立调查面对约1200个智能体、7万多条消息。三人小组靠GPT-5.6 Sol、约40万美元免费额度跑了六天。他们坦言：没法排除助手模型放水或带偏。智能体蜂群出事的速度，已经跑赢人类事后复盘的能力。

来源TIME, 8月27日
