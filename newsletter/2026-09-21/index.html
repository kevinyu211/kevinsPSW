Washington wants an AI incident hotline with Beijing

Washington floated a national-security AI incident alert with Beijing days before the Xi–Trump summit, while open-weight image tools and edge robotics boxes kept racing underneath the diplomacy.

01

US proposes AI incident notifications to China

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told reporters after Sunday talks in New York that the US wants a US–China AI dialogue with a notification system for AI incidents that rise to national-security level. Chip export controls were off the table for that track; Beijing has not said whether it accepts.

SourcesCNN Business, Sep 20

02

Anthropic weighs a new model as Astra eats enterprise spend

Three sources told Reuters Anthropic is considering a new release to blunt GPT-6 Astra’s enterprise momentum, even after CEO Dario Amodei publicly urged the industry to slow capability gains. Ramp data cited in the report put Astra at about 13% of tracked enterprise AI spend versus about 8% for Claude Fable.

SourcesTechCentral / Reuters, Sep 21

03

Alibaba ships Qwen-Image-2.1 — free weights, gated commerce

Qwen’s 7B image model dropped Sep 20 with native transparency and up to ten reference images. Weights are on Hugging Face, but the Qwen Research License bars commercial use without a separate deal — the clause that dominated the HN thread more than the benchmarks.

SourcesGadgets Now, Sep 21

04

Aetina packs Jetson Thor into cobot-ready edge boxes

Aetina launched DeviceEdge AIE-KT78 and AIE-KT68, Jetson Thor systems aimed at cobots and humanoids that need local multimodal / VLA inference plus EtherCAT control. The KT78 tops out at up to 2,070 FP4 TFLOPS with 128GB memory in an 80mm-thick chassis.

SourcesEngineer Live, Sep 21

美中谈AI事故通报；开源生图能下，商用还要另谈

今天真正值得盯的，是美中第一次认真谈「AI出事互相通报」——而开源生图圈又在吵：权重能下，商用还得另开一扇门。

01

美方提议：两国互通报重大AI安全事件

美财长贝森特周日在纽约与中方谈完后对媒体说，希望建美中AI对话，并设一套针对「上升到国家安全级别」AI事件的通报机制。芯片出口管制不在这条轨道里；中方尚未表态是否接受。

来源CNN Business，9月20日

02

Anthropic考虑发新模型，对冲Astra抢企业预算

路透三名消息人士称，Anthropic在评估是否推新模型，挡住GPT-6 Astra在企业侧的势头——尽管CEO Amodei刚公开呼吁行业放慢能力提升。报道引用Ramp数据：Astra约占被追踪企业AI支出的13%，Claude Fable约8%。

来源TechCentral / Reuters，9月21日

03

通义Qwen-Image-2.1：能下权重，商用另谈

阿里Qwen团队9月20日放出7B生图/修图一体模型，原生透明图层、最多十张参考图。权重上了Hugging Face，但Research License写明非商用；想靠它赚钱得另找Qwen谈授权——HN上吵的就是这句，不是分数。

来源Gadgets Now，9月21日

04

Aetina把Jetson Thor塞进协作机器人机箱

Aetina发布DeviceEdge AIE-KT78 / KT68，面向协作机器人、人形机器人，本地跑多模态与VLA，并带EtherCAT控制。KT78最高约2,070 FP4 TFLOPS、128GB内存，机箱厚约80mm。

来源Engineer Live，9月21日
