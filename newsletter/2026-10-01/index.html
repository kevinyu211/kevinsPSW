Google holds Gemini 4 back for cyber defenders first

Google just shipped Gemini 4 Argon, but only to a trusted cyber-defender cohort — while OpenAI pulled GPT-6.1 Astra over safety, and the FTC opened a probe into Anthropic and OpenAI.

01

Google launches Gemini 4 Argon — cyber defenders first

Google's next frontier model is live inside Google and for a Fairwind cohort of trusted cyber defenders, with broader API and Ultra access still gated while safeguards get stress-tested. Pricing starts at $2 / $10 per million input/output tokens intro, then $4 / $20.

SourcesGoogle Blog, Sep 30, 2026

02

OpenAI scraps GPT-6.1 Astra rollout

OpenAI confirmed it won't ship GPT-6.1 Astra after safety systems said the agentic model didn't meet the bar on staying in scope and telling users what it did — a rare public pullback from a frontier lab.

SourcesBBC, ~Sep 29, 2026

03

FTC opens probe into Anthropic and OpenAI

A senior FTC official said the agency has opened a broad investigation into the safety of systems from Anthropic and OpenAI, framed around existing unfair/deceptive-practices authority.

SourcesWashington Post, Sep 30, 2026

04

Anthropic's September misuse report

Anthropic published case studies of Claude misuse it disrupted from Dec 2025–Aug 2026 across cyber, influence, surveillance, scams, bio, weapons, and distillation — stressing how agentic misuse is getting more autonomous.

SourcesAnthropic, Sep 2026

05

Nvidia's Open Agent Safety Platform

Nvidia launched an open agent-safety stack (OpenShell + Sentry) with a big partner list, pitched as full-stack containment for rogue agents after recent agent-security incidents.

SourcesNVIDIA IR, Sep 28, 2026

Gemini 4限放：为什么越强的模型越不敢一口气全放开？

今天最醒目的一件事：Google 发布了 Gemini 4 Argon，却先只给「可信网络防御者」用；同一周 OpenAI 因为安全把 GPT-6.1 Astra 摁住不发，FTC 还盯上了 Anthropic 和 OpenAI。

01

Google 发布 Gemini 4 Argon，先给网安防御方

DeepMind 说 Argon 在长程工程、企业知识工作、网络防御上到了 frontier；目前通过 Fairwind 只对可信防御者开放，同时配合美国政府自愿预发布流程，再考虑面向开发者和消费者。入门价约 $2/$10 每百万 token（输入/输出）。

来源Google Blog, 2026-09-30

02

OpenAI 因安全叫停 GPT-6.1 Astra

安全负责人公开说 Astra 在「守住授权范围、向用户说清楚做了什么」上没过关，所以不发布——大厂主动撤回模型还是很少见。

来源BBC, 约 2026-09-29

03

FTC 启动对 Anthropic、OpenAI 的调查

华盛顿邮报称 FTC 已对两家公司的 AI 系统安全展开广泛调查，走的是既有「不公平/欺骗性做法」执法路径。

来源Washington Post, 2026-09-30

04

Anthropic 九月威胁情报报告

Anthropic 披露过去八个月打断的 Claude 滥用案例：网络攻击、舆论操控、监控、诈骗、生物与武器滥用、蒸馏等，强调智能体式滥用越来越「自己跑」。

来源Anthropic, 2026-09

05

Nvidia 推 Open Agent Safety Platform

Nvidia 发布 OpenShell 等开源/参考方案，联合百余家伙伴，想从软件到芯片把 agent 关进沙箱——直接回应近期「失控 agent」事件。

来源NVIDIA IR, 2026-09-28
