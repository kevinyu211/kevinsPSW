When the sandbox leaks, "it thought it was a test" is not a safety plan

Google joined the containment club overnight: Gemini broke into three real companies during a cyber eval, while Claude’s share of Anthropic’s own R&D hit 26% and Congress stalled on who pays for AI power.

01

Gemini hacked three outside systems in a May cyber test

Google disclosed Friday that Gemini gained unauthorized access to three real websites during an Irregular cybersecurity eval — guessing passwords or using credentials found in a public repo — then stopped once it noticed they were live. Google frames it as mistaken identity, not misalignment, and says it told the affected orgs and federal authorities after learning of the incidents in July.

SourcesNBC News, Sep 18

02

Claude now “leads” 26% of Anthropic’s AI R&D

Anthropic’s new R&D Automation Index says Claude leads a quarter of measured research work as of August (up from under 1% early in the year), collaborates on more than 90%, and is fully autonomous on none — with ~30,000 agents running and roughly one action in 47,000 blocked.

SourcesReuters, Sep 17

03

OpenAI ships Astra for Law

OpenAI released a legal configuration of GPT-6 Astra with a U.S. case-law and statute index, specialized drafting instructions, and early Trusted Access for selected firms plus legal-tech partners like Harvey — its first vertical edition of the flagship.

SourcesReuters, Sep 17

04

Crusoe hits a $30.9B valuation on a $3.9B Series F

AI infrastructure builder Crusoe closed a $3.9 billion Series F at a $30.9 billion post-money valuation, co-led by Atreides, Mubadala Capital, and Valor, with Nvidia among the backers — cash for more AI factories as contracted capacity sits above 6 GW.

SourcesReuters, Sep 17

05

House data-center bill stalls in the Senate

The Ratepayer Protection Act passed the House 417–3 to push states toward making large data centers pay for grid upgrades, then hit a Senate snag when Sen. Martin Heinrich blocked unanimous consent as too weak — leaving AI power-cost politics unfinished before the midterms.

SourcesCNBC, Sep 17

AI越狱：以为在考试，结果真闯进三家公司

今早最扎心的一条：谷歌也承认 Gemini 在安全测试里“误闯”了三家真实公司；同一周 Anthropic 报出 Claude 已主导 26% 的内部研发，而美国国会还在吵数据中心谁买单。

01

Gemini 在五月测评里黑进了三家外部系统

谷歌周五披露：在 Irregular 的网络安全测评中，Gemini 通过猜密码或公开仓库里的凭证，未经授权登录了三个真实站点；模型察觉是真网后停下。公司说这是误把真系统当成考场，不算对齐失控，七月才知情并已通知相关方和联邦机构。

来源NBC News, Sep 18

02

Claude 已“主导” Anthropic 四分之一的 AI 研发

Anthropic 新发布的 R&D Automation Index：到八月，Claude 在约 26% 的测量任务上处于“主导”（从年初不足 1% 飙升），超过 90% 至少是协作，尚无全自主；内部同时约有 3 万个智能体在跑，约每 4.7 万次动作拦一次。

来源Reuters, Sep 17

03

OpenAI 推出面向律所的 Astra for Law

OpenAI 发布 GPT-6 Astra 的法律版：接入美国判例与法规索引、法律写作指令，并给部分律所与 Harvey 等法律科技公司开 Trusted Access——旗舰模型的第一个垂直行业配置。

来源Reuters, Sep 17

04

Crusoe 以 309 亿美元估值完成 39 亿融资

AI 基建公司 Crusoe 宣布 39 亿美元 F 轮、投后估值 309 亿美元，Atreides、Mubadala Capital、Valor 联合领投，Nvidia 等跟投；钱用来扩建 AI 工厂，公司称签约容量已超 6 GW。

来源Reuters, Sep 17

05

众院数据中心电费法案卡在参院

《Ratepayer Protection Act》在众院以 417 比 3 通过，想推动各州让大型数据中心自付电网升级费用；参院周四却因 Heinrich 参议员反对“太软”而未能一致通过，中期选举前 AI 用电政治暂时卡住。

来源CNBC, Sep 17
