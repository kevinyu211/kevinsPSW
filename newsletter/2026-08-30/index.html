When your coding agent's model vendor can walk away overnight

OpenAI is cutting Cursor off after the SpaceX deal, music publishers just piled onto Anthropic, and Google’s conversational video model went GA.

01

OpenAI ends model access for SpaceX-owned Cursor

OpenAI said it will stop providing models to Cursor, with a proposed shutoff of Nov 12, 2026, citing confidence issues after SpaceX’s acquisition and past contract breaches by Musk companies. Anthropic said it will increase compute for Claude inside Cursor. Cursor’s Michael Truell said OpenAI models are about 5% of traffic and talks are ongoing.

SourcesReuters, Aug 28

02

Sony and Warner Chappell sue Anthropic over Claude training

Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell filed in N.D. Cal. alleging torrenting, scraping, and lyric use to train Claude, seeking up to $150k per work plus CMI damages. Co-founders Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann are named as defendants. Anthropic did not immediately comment.

SourcesThe Verge, Aug 29

03

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash goes GA

Google made Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash generally available for developers: scene extension up to 40 seconds, first/last-frame control, cheap 360p drafts, and upscaling to 4K. Preview endpoint retires Sep 30, 2026. Also live in Google Flow for AI Plus/Pro/Ultra.

SourcesGoogle Blog, Aug 27

04

US Commerce drafts rule on remote AI chip access

Commerce is preparing a draft (industry consult expected as early as September) aimed at blocking Chinese AI firms from renting advanced Nvidia GPUs via Southeast Asian cloud. Export-control lawyers say BIS likely lacks clear statutory authority without Congress passing the Remote Access Security Act.

SourcesTechTimes, Aug 29

OpenAI要对Cursor断供，音乐巨头再告Anthropic

今天这波新闻很“硬”：OpenAI要对Cursor断供，音乐巨头再告Anthropic，Google视频模型正式GA，美国还在补算力出口漏洞。

01

OpenAI切断SpaceX旗下Cursor的模型接入

OpenAI宣布计划停止向Cursor提供模型，拟停用日为2026年11月12日，理由是SpaceX收购后无法确信对方会遵守服务条款，并提到马斯克系公司过往违约。Anthropic随后表示会加大Claude在Cursor里的算力支持。Cursor联合创始人Truell称OpenAI模型约占流量5%，双方还在谈。

来源路透社，8月28日

02

索尼、华纳再度起诉Anthropic

索尼音乐出版与华纳Chappell在加州北区起诉，指控用盗版与歌词数据训练Claude，要求最高每件作品15万美元法定赔偿，并点名联合创始人Amodei与Mann。Anthropic暂未回应置评。对做内容/模型的人来说，训练数据溯源又被推到台前。

来源The Verge，8月29日

03

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash正式可用

Google把对话式视频模型Omni 1.1 Flash推向正式版：场景可延长到约40秒、首尾帧控制、便宜的360p草稿，以及最高4K放大。旧预览接口将在9月30日下线；Flow订阅用户也能用。

来源Google Blog，8月27日

04

美国商务部起草“远程租卡”新规

商务部准备就中国公司通过东南亚机房远程租用英伟达高端GPU起草规则，最早九月征求业界意见。但出口管制律师普遍认为，若国会不过《远程访问安全法》，现有法律对“远程算力”约束力很弱。

来源TechTimes，8月29日
