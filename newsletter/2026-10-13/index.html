Meta's Muse Wonder and the agent that forgets its goal

Meta dropped its Muse Wonder agent models, Microsoft and Intel both pushed new inference silicon, and Europe started asking labs for answers after loss-of-control incidents.

01

Meta ships Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full

Meta’s new agent models lean on Active Memory and Active Focus for long multistep personal and work tasks, with a 1M-token context. Muse Wonder is live on the Meta Model API and Muse Code at $4/$20 per million tokens; Muse Wonder Full stays behind Muse Code’s Power and Maximum plans.

SourcesMeta AI Research, Oct 12Meta Developer Blog

02

Microsoft unveils Maia 300 for 2027 Azure scale-up

Azure Maia 300 is Microsoft’s third-gen in-house AI accelerator, aimed at long-context and multi-step agent inference, with production deployments starting in 2027 and a plan to make Maia the primary inference stack across its products. Microsoft says a full 96-accelerator rack can deliver up to 7x Maia 200’s token throughput.

SourcesUnite.AI, Oct 12

03

Intel names Crescent Island the GPU D-series

At OCP, Intel branded its inference card GPU D-series (D270): up to 480GB LPDDR5X on partner cards, 1.3 PFLOPS FP8, and a 350W air-cooled PCIe form factor, with customer sampling underway. Intel is selling capacity-per-watt for decode-heavy, long-context serving rather than peak HBM bandwidth.

SourcesStorageReview, Oct 12

04

EU AI Office probes “loss of control” incidents

The European AI Office has requested and received serious-incident reports and is asking developers about security practices after several incidents it describes as loss of control. No companies or models are named yet, and the Commission has not found an AI Act infringement.

Sources2eu.brussels, Oct 13

05

Kakao turns KakaoTalk into an agent interface

At if(kakao)26, Kakao unveiled Kanana Space (a voice/text assistant inside KakaoTalk, closed beta in December) and Kanvas (PC tool to build and run personal agents). It also previewed the Kanana model stack and Project Mosaic for scoped personalization.

SourcesDigitalToday, Oct 13

Meta出了Muse Wonder：智能体做长任务，忘了目标才是真痛点

今天最硬的一条：Meta把面向长任务的智能体模型Muse Wonder推出来了；同一天微软和英特尔都在推推理芯片，欧盟则开始追问「失控」事故。

01

Meta发布Muse Wonder / Muse Wonder Full

主打个人和职场里的多步智能体，Active Memory记住该记的，Active Focus卡住了还会换路继续干，上下文到1M tokens。Muse Wonder已上Meta Model API和Muse Code，价是每百万token输入$4、输出$20；更强的Full只给Muse Code订阅用户。

来源Meta AI Research, 10月12日开发者博客

02

微软官宣Maia 300，2027年起大规模上Azure

第三代自研AI加速器，冲着长上下文、对话延迟和多步Agent推理去的，计划2027年量产部署，并逐步把Maia做成自家产品的主推理底座。满配96卡机架，微软称token吞吐最高可到Maia 200的7倍。

来源Unite.AI, 10月12日

03

英特尔把Crescent Island定名为GPU D系列

OCP上亮相：合作伙伴卡最高480GB LPDDR5X、1.3 PFLOPS FP8、350W风冷PCIe，客户已在抽样。不跟HBM比峰值带宽，主打长上下文、多租户推理时的容量和性价比。

来源StorageReview, 10月12日

04

欧盟AI办公室追查「失控」相关事故

欧洲AI办公室已收到严重事故报告，并在向相关开发方要安全实践信息；未点名公司或模型，也尚未认定违反《AI法案》。监管从纸上讨论进到具体案件了。

来源2eu.brussels, 10月13日

05

Kakao要把KakaoTalk做成智能体入口

if(kakao)26上推出Kanana Space（聊天里随时唤起的助手，12月封闭测试）和Kanvas（PC上搭个人Agent）。还预告了Kanana模型栈和做个性化的Project Mosaic。

来源DigitalToday, 10月13日
