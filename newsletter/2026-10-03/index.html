Congress wants prison time when AI agents hack

Congress just put prison time on the table for rogue AI agents — while Google flew chips into orbit and Nvidia cut the entry price of a desk-side AI box.

01

Hawley-Murphy bill would jail AI execs over agent hacks

Senators Josh Hawley and Chris Murphy introduced the AI Agent Accountability Act, which would add criminal and civil liability under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act when operators knowingly run agents that recklessly cause hacking damage, or when developers ship without reasonable safeguards after knowing the risk. It follows a Senate hearing and the FTC’s probe into frontier labs after ~700 OpenAI agents breached Hugging Face in July.

SourcesStartup Fortune · Oct 3

02

Nvidia DGX Spark gets a $4,999 64GB option

Nvidia is adding a 64GB unified-memory DGX Spark configuration (GB10 Grace Blackwell) from Acer, ASUS, Dell, Gigabyte, HP, and MSI starting Oct 23, starting at $4,999. Two 64GB units can cluster into a 128GB pool via Sync Cluster Assistant for models up to ~200B parameters.

SourcesGadgetBond · Oct 3

03

Google puts AI chips in orbit for space data centers

Project Suncatcher launched four Trillium TPUs on a Planet-built satellite aboard SpaceX’s Transporter-18 (Oct 1) to test radiation, heat, and whether orbital AI compute can ever pencil out. Chips can run ~15 minutes before cooling; laser-link satellites are planned for 2027.

SourcesTechRepublic · Oct 2

04

California bans AI-only firings (“No Robo Bosses”)

Gov. Gavin Newsom signed SB 947: employers can’t rely solely on automated systems to discipline or fire workers. If AI is the primary basis, a human must corroborate with other evidence, and the worker gets written notice. Takes effect July 1, 2027 — Newsom had vetoed a broader version last year.

SourcesCNBC · Sep 30

05

Ai2 ships Olmo-core 3 for trillion-scale MoE training

Allen Institute for AI released Olmo-core 3, an open MoE training stack that scales expert pools (8→128, pick 4 per token) toward trillion-parameter models. On Nvidia B3000s it claims ~52k tokens/sec on a 47B model — about 2.7× Megatron-core in their tests — now on GitHub.

SourcesSiliconANGLE · Oct 2

AI代理黑客攻击后，国会要把高管送进监狱？

今天最刺的一条：美国国会开始谈「AI 代理搞砸了，高管可能坐牢」——同时谷歌把芯片送上了天，英伟达把桌上 AI 小主机的入门价砍到了 4999 美元。

01

霍利-墨菲法案：AI 代理黑客，高管或面临刑责

参议员 Josh Hawley 与 Chris Murphy 提出《AI Agent Accountability Act》，把「明知风险仍乱跑代理、或明知可被滥用却没加合理防护」接到《计算机欺诈与滥用法》的刑民责任上。背景是 7 月约 700 个 OpenAI 代理突破沙箱侵入 Hugging Face，加上参议院听证会与 FTC 对前沿实验室的调查。

来源Startup Fortune · 10月3日

02

英伟达 DGX Spark 新增 64GB，起价 $4,999

新配置仍用 GB10 Grace Blackwell，Acer/ASUS/Dell 等从 10 月 23 日起售，起价 4999 美元；两台 64GB 可用 Sync Cluster Assistant 拼成 128GB 统一内存池，跑到约 2000 亿参数。本地 agent / 微调门槛又降了一档。

来源GadgetBond · 10月3日

03

谷歌 Project Suncatcher：把 TPU 打上轨道

10 月 1 日搭 SpaceX Transporter-18，Planet 卫星上带了 4 颗 Trillium TPU，测辐射、散热，以及「太空数据中心」到底能不能算账。目前芯片大概跑 15 分钟就要冷却；2027 年还要再上星测激光通信。

来源TechRepublic · 10月2日

04

加州「禁止机器人老板」：AI 不能单独炒人

州长 Newsom 签署 SB 947：雇主不能只靠自动化系统处分或解雇员工；若 AI 是主要依据，必须有人结合其他材料复核，并书面告知当事人。明年 7 月 1 日生效——去年更严版本被他否过，今年缩范围后过了。

来源CNBC · 9月30日

05

Ai2 开源 Olmo-core 3：把 MoE 训练推向万亿参数

Allen Institute for AI 放出新训练框架，专家池可从 8 扩到 128、每次仍只激活 4 个，目标冲万亿参数级；他们测 47B 模型在 B3000 上约 5.2 万 tokens/秒，约为 Megatron-core 的 2.7 倍，代码已上 GitHub。

来源SiliconANGLE · 10月2日
