Agents are now an API

Safety reports and agent plumbing moved faster than the weekend news cycle.

01

Anthropic’s threat report: AI is already running parts of the kill chain

Anthropic’s September 2026 threat intel covers Dec 2025–Aug 2026 misuse across cyber ops, influence campaigns, surveillance, fraud, bio, weapons, and distillation. The through-line: actors used Haiku/Sonnet/Opus as orchestrators (recon, phishing, malware rebuild loops), not just chat helpers — and Anthropic says it disrupted the campaigns and shared intel with partners.

SourcesAnthropic · Sept 2026

02

OpenAI ships Agents API (public beta)

OpenAI put the Codex-style harness behind a public Agents API on Sept 10 — managed sessions, tool use, subagents, plus hosted or bring-your-own sandboxes. No separate Agents API fee; you pay for tokens, tools, and (if you use them) hosted sandbox containers.

SourcesOpenAI · Sept 10, 2026

03

ChatGPT for Financial Services goes live

OpenAI launched a finance-flavored ChatGPT for banks and investment firms, built with Morgan Stanley and Evercore and wired to licensed data from S&P Capital IQ, LSEG, PitchBook, Moody’s, and more. TNW flags the missing EU data-residency / DORA detail that European buyers will still have to negotiate.

SourcesThe Next Web · Sept 11, 2026

04

Washington pressure rises; OpenAI flips toward mandatory rules

After Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon resigned warning labs are racing irresponsibly — with Evan Hubinger backing him — more US lawmakers are pushing new AI rules. Same window: OpenAI’s Chris Lehane urged Congress to pass compulsory frontier safety laws before December, a clear shift off “voluntary commitments only.”

SourcesThe Japan Times · Sept 11, 2026Mathrubhumi · Sept 11, 2026

01

Anthropic 威胁情报：AI 已在帮黑客跑流程

Anthropic 发布 2026 年 9 月威胁情报报告，覆盖 2025 年 12 月到 2026 年 8 月、七类滥用场景（网络攻击、舆论操控、监控、诈骗、生物、武器、蒸馏等）。重点不是「AI 会写恶意代码」，而是威胁方把 Claude 当成编排器：侦察、钓鱼、改马、并行代理一起上；Anthropic 称已打断并与合作方共享情报。

来源Anthropic · 2026年9月

02

OpenAI Agents API 公测：Codex 那套 harness 对外开放

9 月 10 日，OpenAI 把驱动 Codex 的 harness 做成 Agents API 公测：管会话、工具调用、子代理，还能选官方托管沙箱或自带环境。没有单独的 Agents API 附加费，按 token、工具，以及（若用）托管沙箱计费。

来源OpenAI · 2026年9月10日

03

ChatGPT 金融版上线：模型 + 行情数据一起卖

OpenAI 推出面向银行与投行的 ChatGPT for Financial Services，和摩根士丹利、Evercore 一起打磨，并接入 S&P Capital IQ、LSEG、PitchBook、Moody's 等授权数据。欧洲买家还会追问数据驻留和 DORA——TNW 指出官宣里几乎没写清楚。

来源The Next Web · 2026年9月11日

04

美国监管升温，OpenAI 转向「要强制规则」

Anthropic 研究员 Jacob Coxon 辞职并警告实验室在不负责任地竞速，Evan Hubinger 公开附和后，更多国会议员呼吁新规。同一时段，OpenAI 全球事务负责人 Chris Lehane 呼吁国会在 12 月休会前通过强制性前沿安全立法——等于从「自愿承诺够了」翻盘。
