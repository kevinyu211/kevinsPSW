Independent benchmarks cool OpenAI's AGI announcement

The morning after Astra’s AGI-era press conference, independent benches cooled the claim, and Washington kept talking past itself on Anthropic and who gets to regulate.

01

Independent scoreboard: Astra is flat on general intelligence, pricey, strong on coding

Artificial Analysis put GPT-6 Astra at 61 on its Intelligence Index — tied with GPT-5.6 Sol and behind Claude Fable 5.1. On the Coding Agent Index it hits 67, roughly even with Fable 5 / Opus 5, with big token-efficiency gains. API price is 2.5x Sol ($10/$50 per million in/out), so at max effort AA says it’s about 75% more expensive per intelligence task despite similar scores.

SourcesArtificial Analysis · Sep 3

02

Commerce says Anthropic is “in tune.” The Pentagon says it’s still a supply-chain risk

A day after Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said Anthropic and the government were aligned, Pentagon R&E chief Emil Michael posted that Anthropic “is still a designated supply chain risk.” Legal experts call the split contradictory; the D.C. Circuit fight over a separate designation is still live after Judge Lin’s late-August win for Anthropic in California.

SourcesFedScoop · Sep 4

03

Zuckerberg privately pushed back on a FINRA-style AI regulator

Politico, via TNW: Zuckerberg raised concerns in a mid-August call with Trump about a proposed national AI reviewer modelled on FINRA. Live options are that industry-funded body or an even lighter MPA-style voluntary ratings scheme from David Sacks — so the fight is how much self-regulation, not whether.

SourcesTNW · Sep 4

04

Meta hit with a new Illinois BIPA class action over smart-glasses AI

A proposed class action in Northern District of Illinois says Meta harvested Facebook/Instagram images without consent to train facial-recognition and generative AI for Ray-Ban and Oakley smart glasses, allegedly violating Illinois’ biometric privacy law.

SourcesBloomberg Law · Sep 4

独立榜把OpenAI的AGI话术浇冷了

Astra 喊完「AGI 时代」的第二天，独立评测把分数浇冷了；华盛顿这边一边跟 Anthropic 示好，一边还把它当供应链风险。

01

独立榜：Astra 综合能力几乎没涨，贵了 2.5 倍，写代码倒是真强

Artificial Analysis 给 GPT-6 Astra 的 Intelligence Index 打了 61，和上一代 GPT-5.6 Sol 持平，落后 Claude Fable 5.1。编码代理指数到 67，大致追平 Fable 5 / Opus 5，token 效率高很多。API 价涨到 $10/$50（百万 in/out），是 Sol 的 2.5 倍；按 AA 说法，满努力下每项综合任务大约贵 75%。

来源Artificial Analysis · 9月3日

02

商务部说 Anthropic「对上了」，五角大楼说它还是供应链风险

商务部长 Lutnick 刚说 Anthropic 和政府「in tune」，国防部研发负责人 Emil Michael 第二天发帖：公司「仍是指定的供应链风险」。法律学者直接说这矛盾；加州法官八月底判 Anthropic 胜诉之后，D.C. 巡回法院里另一套指定还在打。

来源FedScoop · 9月4日

03

扎克伯格私下反对 FINRA 式 AI 监管机构

TNW 转述 Politico：八月中旬扎克伯格跟特朗普通话，对仿 FINRA 的国家级 AI 预审机构表达担忧。桌上还剩两条路——行业出钱的审查机构，或 Sacks 推的更轻的电影分级式自愿方案。争论点不是要不要「监管」，是自我监管要多紧。

来源TNW · 9月4日

04

Meta 智能眼镜再吃伊利诺伊生物识别集体诉讼

北伊利诺伊联邦法院新起诉称：Meta 未经同意抓取 Facebook / Instagram 图像，训练 Ray-Ban、Oakley 智能眼镜的人脸识别和生成式 AI，涉嫌违反伊利诺伊生物识别隐私法（BIPA）。

来源Bloomberg Law · 9月4日
