Privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

YE stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the You Are Extinct YouTube channel. It uses Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API with read-only access to that channel.

What it accesses

Channel and video statistics (views, likes, comments, subscriber counts) and channel analytics (watch time, average view duration and percentage, subscribers gained and lost). It does not access email, contacts, files, or any other Google data, and it does not upload, edit or delete anything.

How the data is used

The numbers are used only to produce reports for the channel owner about how the channel's videos perform. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Access credentials are stored privately by the channel owner. Reports are kept only in the owner's private workspace. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YE stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
