Thursday, September 17, 2026·Chips·2 min read

Cyber risk and chip shortages hit the same morning

Chips and control dominated overnight: Huawei said it still can’t meet China AI demand while pulling Ascend 960 forward, a watchdog mapped how restricted Nvidia GPUs keep reaching China anyway, and OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra became the first model tagged Critical for cyber risk as a key senator pushes year-end safety rules.

01

Huawei pulls Ascend 960 forward, still can’t meet demand

At Huawei Connect in Shanghai, rotating chairs said Ascend 960DT is due in Q1 2027 and 960PR in Q3 — both earlier than planned — while Eric Xu said capacity isn’t enough for China alone and overseas sales stay limited. Xu also claimed Ascend’s China share may already top Nvidia’s, without offering hard data.

SourcesReuters, Sep 17

02

GPT-6 Astra hits Critical cyber — and lands in Microsoft Foundry

InfoQ reports OpenAI classified GPT-6 Astra at Critical cybersecurity under its Preparedness Framework, the first model to clear that bar, with Microsoft making it generally available in Foundry the same day. OpenAI says Astra is live in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the API, with new admin controls for computer use.

SourcesInfoQ, Sep 17

03

Senator Warner: pass AI safety standards by year-end

Senate Intelligence vice chair Mark Warner told Reuters Congress should lock in AI safety protocols before January, calling extinction talk overdone but warning autonomous agents could hit banks, water, or hospitals. Republicans and Trump remain wary of heavy federal rules ahead of a Trump–Xi meeting on Sep 24.

SourcesReuters, Sep 17

04

Report: billions in restricted Nvidia chips still reach China

Tom’s Hardware covers a C4ADS investigation mapping research-institution buys, Southeast Asia transshipment, and shell companies — including about $4.6B tied to one opaque importer — as Epoch AI has estimated that a large share of China’s AI compute may be smuggled GPUs.

SourcesTom’s Hardware, Sep 17

华为产能不够，Nvidia禁令也挡不住

今早最硬的一条线是算力：华为说昇腾还供不上国内、960还要提前一年出；同时有报告写禁运的Nvidia芯片仍大量进中国。另一边，OpenAI的GPT-6 Astra首次被标成网络安全Critical，华府也在催年底前立安全规矩。

01

华为：960提前，国内需求还吃不饱

华为全联接大会上，轮值董事长说Ascend 960DT计划2027年一季度、960PR三季度上市，都比原计划更早；徐直军称产能连国内都不够，海外只做有限供应，并口头判断昇腾在中国份额可能已超Nvidia，但没给数据。

来源路透社，9月17日

02

GPT-6 Astra：首次Critical网络安全等级，同日进Foundry

InfoQ报道，OpenAI按Preparedness Framework把GPT-6 Astra标到网络安全Critical——这是首个摸到该门槛的模型；微软同日在Foundry上线。OpenAI称Astra已在ChatGPT Work、Codex和API可用，并加了企业端电脑使用管控。

来源InfoQ，9月17日

03

参议员Warner：年底前先立AI安全标准

参议院情报委员会副主席Mark Warner对路透说，国会应在明年1月前先通过一套AI安全协议；他不认「人类灭绝」叙事，但担心智能体冲进银行、供水、医院。共和党与特朗普仍忌讳重监管，9月24日特朗普–习会前这题会更烫。

来源路透社，9月17日

04

调查：禁运Nvidia芯片仍绕道进中国

Tom’s Hardware梳理C4ADS报告：科研机构采购、东南亚转运、壳公司是三条主路；仅一家权属不清的进口商就关联约46亿美元规模。Epoch AI此前估计，中国AI算力里相当一部分可能来自走私GPU。

来源Tom’s Hardware，9月17日
