A bit about me

From zero code to a company of one.

I'm a Shanghai American School alum, and now I study biomedical engineering at Purdue, with a background in statistics. I want to end up in healthcare AI. Right now I'm on a gap year until spring 2027, building full time.

In mid-2024 I couldn't code. I learned by building with AI, and one rule has stuck: if a general chatbot can already do it, I don't build it. Whatever survives that test, I spec carefully and ship.

I work in English and Mandarin, between Canada, the US, China and Hong Kong.
