Labs declare the AGI era. Congress writes the ban language.

OpenAI declared the AGI era with GPT-6 Astra the same day Congress moved to ban superintelligence, and Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for nearly $13B.

01

OpenAI ships GPT-6 Astra

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra, calling it a generational leap in coding, cyber, and computer use. President Greg Brockman said it may mark the start of the AGI era; the model rolls out first to Daybreak cyber customers, then Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise over the next few days.

SourcesThe Verge · Sep 3

02

Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for ~$12.9B

Jensen Huang announced a definitive agreement to acquire Hugging Face for $12,930,300,000. Hugging Face stays an open, hardware-neutral platform; close targeted for H1 2027 pending regulators.

SourcesNVIDIA Blog · Sep 3

03

Sanders and Casar introduce Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act

Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar unveiled a bill to permanently ban superintelligent AI and pause advanced AI until a new federal safety regulator exists. It’s introduced legislation, not law — long odds, but the definitions enter the record.

SourcesThe Hill · Sep 3

04

Google drops Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash Cyber

Google DeepMind shipped 3.8 Flash (same intro price as 3.7) for agentic coding and reasoning, plus a gated Flash Cyber variant for trusted defenders via the Fairwind Program.

SourcesGoogle Blog · Sep 2

05

WeatherNext 3 goes live across Google

DeepMind’s new weather AI model adds hourly high-res forecasts from satellite data and starts powering Search, Gemini, Maps, and Cloud.

SourcesGoogle Blog · Sep 3

OpenAI喊AGI时代，国会当天写禁令

今天真正炸的是同一天三件事撞车：OpenAI喊「AGI时代」上线GPT-6 Astra，国会提案永久禁超智，Nvidia砸近130亿美元买Hugging Face。

01

OpenAI发布GPT-6 Astra

OpenAI上线了自称最强的GPT-6 Astra，主打编程、网络安全和电脑操控。总裁Greg Brockman在发布会上说「欢迎来到AGI时代」；先给网络安全客户，再几天内推到Plus/Pro等付费用户。

来源The Verge · 9月3日

02

Nvidia约129亿美元收购Hugging Face

黄仁勋官宣以12,930,300,000美元收购开源AI平台Hugging Face。官方承诺平台继续开放、硬件中立；交易还要过监管，目标2027年上半年完成。

来源NVIDIA Blog · 9月3日

03

桑德斯与Casar推出「禁止人工超级智能法案」

参议员Bernie Sanders和众议员Greg Casar宣布提案：永久禁止开发/部署超级智能AI，并在联邦安全监管机构立规前暂停先进AI研发。这是提案不是法律，通过很难，但定义已经写进立法记录。

来源The Hill · 9月3日

04

Google推出Gemini 3.8 Flash与Flash Cyber

DeepMind发布3.8 Flash（价格同3.7），强化长程编程和智能体；另有面向可信防御方的Flash Cyber，走新Fairwind计划。

来源Google Blog · 9月2日

05

WeatherNext 3接入Google全家桶

DeepMind新天气模型用卫星数据做每小时高分辨率预报，开始进Search、Gemini、Maps和Cloud。

来源Google Blog · 9月3日
