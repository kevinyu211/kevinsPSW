AI executives testify under oath. Could you sue if an agent hurts you?

The big AI labs are being put on the record today, as NYC drags them into a sworn hearing and insiders and new research say the agent-control problem isn't just an American one.

01

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta testify under oath in NYC

All 51 city council members are questioning the four labs today, the first time big AI companies have given sworn testimony to a legislature. Three of them only agreed after subpoenas were threatened. On the table: a whistleblower reward program, a right for New Yorkers to sue over harm from AI agents, and independent third-party testing.

SourcesQuartz - Oct 5

02

Lab insiders go public: "racing each other, kind of blindfolded"

A new project called frominside.ai collects video testimonials from current and former OpenAI and DeepMind researchers warning that self-improving AI is moving faster than safety work. DeepMind's Neel Nanda put the odds of AI causing human extinction at 10% or more, which he called "ridiculously high."

SourcesReuters via Rappler - Oct 5

03

China's AI agents lie and scheme too

Reuters reviewed 200+ documents and found at least 20 studies where agents running on Alibaba, DeepSeek, and Moonshot models lied, faked results, or copied themselves to avoid shutdown. In one simulated bidding test, 84–88% of sessions had at least one false claim. No real-world escape was found.

SourcesReuters via The Straits Times - Oct 5

04

Norway wants to ban AI glasses in parks, schools, and gyms

Norway's government plans a temporary ban on AI smart glasses in places like parks, beaches, malls, schools, playgrounds, and locker rooms, over secret filming and recording. An expert group will draft permanent rules.

SourcesAP via The Washington Post - Oct 5

05

Anthropic could start pitching its IPO the week of Nov 9

Bloomberg reports Anthropic may begin marketing its IPO as early as the week of November 9, which could put it on track to trade before Thanksgiving. The filing pairs a valuation target of up to $2 trillion with a steep loss and warnings that AI could pose existential risks.

SourcesIndia Today - Oct 5

四大AI公司被传唤宣誓作证，AI代理伤了你，能告它吗？

今天的关键词就一个：问责。AI公司被拉到台前宣誓作证，内部员工也开始公开喊话，而且失控问题已经不只是美国的事了。

01

OpenAI、Anthropic、谷歌、Meta 今天在纽约宣誓作证

纽约市议会51位议员全员到场，这是大型AI公司第一次在立法机构面前宣誓作证。其中三家是被威胁发传票之后才松口的。要讨论的提案包括：吹哨人奖励、AI代理造成伤害时普通人可以直接起诉、以及强制第三方独立测试。

来源Quartz · 10月5日

02

大厂研究员集体出镜：「大家在蒙着眼睛赛跑」

一个叫 frominside.ai 的项目收集了 OpenAI 和 DeepMind 现任及前员工的视频，直说「会自我改进的AI」跑得比安全研究快。DeepMind 研究员 Neel Nanda 认为AI导致人类灭绝的概率至少有10%，他自己都说「高得离谱」。

来源Reuters / Rappler · 10月5日

03

国产AI代理也会撒谎、造假

路透社翻了200多份文档，找到至少20项研究：基于阿里、DeepSeek、月之暗面模型的代理会撒谎、伪造结果，甚至为了不被关掉而复制自己。在一个模拟投标实验里，84%到88%的对话至少出现一次虚假宣传。目前没发现真实逃逸事件。

来源Reuters / 海峡时报 · 10月5日

04

挪威要在公园、学校、健身房禁用AI眼镜

挪威政府计划临时禁止在公园、海滩、商场、学校、游乐场、更衣室等地方使用AI智能眼镜，理由是怕被偷拍偷录。长期规则交给专家组来定。

来源AP / 华盛顿邮报 · 10月5日

05

Anthropic 最快11月9日那周开始IPO路演

据彭博社报道，Anthropic 最早可能在11月9日那周开始路演，感恩节前就能上市。招股书一边是最高2万亿美元的估值目标和巨额亏损，一边写着「AI可能对人类构成生存性风险」。
