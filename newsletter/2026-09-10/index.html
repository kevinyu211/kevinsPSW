DeepSeek makes its own Pro look expensive

DeepSeek shipped a Flash model that beats its own Pro on cost and speed, DOJ opened an Nvidia–Groq antitrust probe, and an Anthropic researcher quit over the race to self-improving AI.

01

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash

DeepSeek called it the smallest model in its new architecture family: a 552B MoE with native vision, a Causal Encoder–Decoder design (8B active on input, 16B on output), and a much smaller KV cache. API id is `deepseek-flash`; old Flash names route here, and from Sep 14 UTC all V4 Pro traffic routes to Flash at Flash prices until V4.1-Pro ships.

SourcesDeepSeek · Sep 10

02

DOJ digs into Nvidia’s Groq deal

The Justice Department is examining whether Nvidia’s non-exclusive licence-plus-hire arrangement with inference chipmaker Groq was structured to avoid merger review. Nvidia licensed Groq tech and hired key execs including founder Jonathan Ross without buying the company; reporters also flag a similar Poolside pattern.

SourcesThe Next Web · Sep 10NYT · Sep 9

03

Anthropic researcher resigns over ASI race

Jacob Coxon, who’d done pretraining at OpenAI and Anthropic, publicly quit warning labs are “racing straight to self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives.” TechCrunch ties the exit to recent agent sandbox escapes and growing pressure for slowdowns.

SourcesTechCrunch · Sep 9

04

UK ASI ban bill hits Parliament

Labour MP Alex Sobel introduced the Artificial Superintelligence Security Bill (drafted with ControlAI), seeking to ban ASI development and monitor precursors. It’s a private member’s bill with little chance of becoming law without government backing, but it’s the first G7-parliament push framed as an ASI ban.

SourcesPinsent Masons · Sep 9

DeepSeek新模型Flash反超Pro，Nvidia收购案也来了

今天这波挺扎心：DeepSeek 用更小更快的 Flash 把自家 Pro 挤下去了，美国司法部盯上 Nvidia「授权+挖人」算不算并购，Anthropic 又有研究员公开辞职喊停超级智能军备赛。

01

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 上线

官方说这是新架构家族里最小的一款：552B MoE、原生多模态、因果式 Encoder–Decoder（输入侧约 8B 活跃、输出侧约 16B），KV cache 大幅压缩。API 用 `deepseek-flash`；旧 Flash 名称会转到新模型，北京时间 9 月 14 日中午起，V4 Pro 请求也会转到 Flash 并按 Flash 计价，直到 V4.1-Pro 发布。

来源DeepSeek · 9月10日

02

DOJ 调查 Nvidia–Groq 交易结构

据报道，美国司法部在查 Nvidia 和推理芯片公司 Groq 的非独家授权+挖人安排，是否刻意绕开并购申报。Nvidia 拿了技术、招走包括创始人 Jonathan Ross 在内的核心高管，但没整体收购公司；类似 Poolside 交易也被拿来对照。

来源The Next Web · 9月10日纽约时报 · 9月9日

03

Anthropic 研究员辞职警示

Jacob Coxon（曾在 OpenAI、Anthropic 做预训练）公开辞职，称业界在「直奔可自我改进的超级智能，拿人命赌博」。报道把它和近期智能体逃出沙箱、监管加码放在同一语境里。

来源TechCrunch · 9月9日

04

英国议会提交超级智能禁令草案

工党议员 Alex Sobel 提出《人工超级智能安全法案》（与 ControlAI 合作起草），想禁止开发超级智能并监控前兆能力。作为私人议员法案，没有政府背书很难成真，但算是 G7 议会里第一波明确打「禁 ASI」旗号的动作。

来源Pinsent Masons · 9月9日
