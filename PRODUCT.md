# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who meets Kevin or hears about his work: hiring managers, potential consulting clients, collaborators and people who follow what he builds. Kevin chose not to narrow it to one audience (2026-11-20). They arrive from a link (LinkedIn, X, WeChat, a résumé) and decide in under a minute whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

Kevin Yu's personal website: personal brand, portfolio and résumé in one. It shows what he builds and ships, then makes it easy to get in touch. Success: a visitor understands what Kevin does from the visuals alone, opens one or two projects, and then contacts him, follows him or subscribes.

## Positioning

A Purdue biomedical engineering student on a gap year who went from no code in mid-2024 to shipping real products across very different worlds: a startup (Hirvo, co-founder and CTO), client software (a live B2B risk tool, a WeChat mini program, a real estate site with 3D), iOS apps and games, nine children's books on Amazon, AI films, and healthcare AI hackathon builds. He builds by directing AI coding agents.

## Operating Context

- Visitors land from links on phones and laptops, in English or Chinese contexts.
- Kevin works in English and Mandarin, across Canada, the US, China and Hong Kong.
- The Daily AI newsletter (bilingual, 42 issues to Oct 14, 2026) lives on the site. Kevin wants it to keep publishing so the site always has fresh news. Issues come from a Notion database, built by `tools/build_newsletter.py`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on Vercel from `main` at kevinpeiyuanyu.com. GSAP, ScrollTrigger, Flip and Lenis are vendored.
- Contact actions wanted: consulting enquiries, LinkedIn, X (handle not live yet), newsletter subscribe, WeChat and phone.
- Client work must stay anonymous: no client, customer or staff names, no private URLs.
- Open: the X handle, an email address for enquiries, and a subscribe mechanism for the newsletter.

## Brand Commitments

- Kevin pinned the feel (2026-11-20): minimal, calm and professional. He likes Swiss design and an editorial feel, plus the restraint of Japanese minimalism without Japanese theming. Less text, more visuals and animation. Projects as categories you click into for detail. No "AI slop".
- Names: Kevin Yu, Peiyuan Yu, 余佩远.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: Amazon book listings (9 titles, ASINs in `index.html`), YouTube films and channels (YouAreExtinct, Noir Hours, Snuggle Crew read-along), AI influencer media in `assets/media/`, the WeChat QR in `assets/img/`, the public Hirvo app, the UHOME demo, the newsletter archive.
- Coming from Kevin later: project screenshots, UI/UX animations, account pages.
- Absent, never to be invented: testimonials, client logos, revenue, user counts beyond what the project notes state.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't tell: every project leads with a visual, and the words come after a click.
2. Real over illustrated: real screenshots, covers and video beat drawn mockups.
3. Honest status: label what is live, in testing or planned, and never inflate traction.
4. One clear next step on every screen: open a project, or get in touch.
