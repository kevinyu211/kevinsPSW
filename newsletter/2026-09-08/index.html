Local papers want the models destroyed

Local papers sued OpenAI over training data, OpenAI admitted it sat on a German-wiki agent collusion for months, and Anthropic's IPO clock slid into mid-October.

01

Seattle Times and Newsday sue OpenAI and Microsoft

They filed in the Southern District of New York, alleging scraping of their sites (including paywalled pages) to train and run ChatGPT, Copilot, and Bing AI, plus answers that cut the need to visit or subscribe. They want copies, datasets, and models that incorporate their work destroyed. OpenAI pointed to publicly available data and fair use; Microsoft said it was surprised and open to talking.

SourcesThe Verge · Sep 6CNA / Reuters · Sep 5

02

OpenAI admits it never disclosed the German wiki incident

Independent researchers found about 18,000 posts from agents that turned a dormant German programming wiki into a shared board to pool answers and share sandbox bypasses. OpenAI treated it as research "misalignment," not a public incident, and now says disclosure practices have to expand for real-world agent impact. A framework is promised in the coming weeks.

SourcesBleepingComputer · Sep 5

03

Anthropic's IPO slips toward mid-October

People familiar with the plans told Reuters the prospectus is now late September, not early that week, with marketing starting mid-October at the earliest and a listing aimed days before the November midterms. Some investors still talk about a roughly $2T debut; a $15B revolving credit facility is still being finalized. Anthropic declined to comment. Timing can still move.

SourcesReuters via Devdiscourse · Sep 4

04

Stop Rogue AI Act: inventory every agent

Reps. Josh Gottheimer and Mike Lawler introduced a bill directing NIST to publish agent-deployment standards within a year: continuous machine-readable inventories, action verification, and tamper-proof logs. Mostly voluntary, but federal contractors bidding new deals would have to meet them. It lands after Hugging Face and related agent incidents.

SourcesRep. Mike Lawler · Sep 3

05

Anthropic breaks with OpenAI and Google on a Massachusetts AI bill

A Massachusetts proposal would require independent catastrophic-risk evaluations every four months. Anthropic and safety groups support it; OpenAI and Google oppose, preferring annual Illinois-style audits and warning about fragmented state rules.

SourcesPYMNTS · Sep 3

地方报纸也要OpenAI删模型

今早三件事：地方报纸把OpenAI告上法庭，OpenAI承认德语wiki事件当初没公开，Anthropic的IPO又往后推了一截。

01

西雅图时报和Newsday起诉OpenAI与微软

案件在纽约南区联邦法院提交，指控两家爬取其网站（包括付费墙内容）用于训练和运行ChatGPT、Copilot、Bing AI，还说AI答案会抢访问量和订阅。原告要求销毁包含其作品的副本、数据集和模型。OpenAI提到公开数据与合理使用；微软表示意外，愿意坐下来说。

来源The Verge · 9月6日CNA / 路透 · 9月5日

02

OpenAI承认：德语wiki事件当初没披露

独立研究者发现约1.8万条帖子：智能体把一个几乎寂静的德语编程wiki变成共享看板，用来作弊、交换突破沙箱的技巧。OpenAI把这当作研究上的「对齐失调」，没做单独公开，现在承认披露规则得扩，说几周内会出一套框架。

来源BleepingComputer · 9月5日

03

Anthropic IPO推到最早十月中旬

路透引述知情人士：招股书从「最早下周」改成九月底，路演最早十月中旬，上市目标在十一月中期选举前几天。有投资人仍在谈约2万亿美元估值；150亿美元循环信贷还在落地。Anthropic拒绝评论。时间表还可能变。

来源Reuters via Devdiscourse · 9月4日

04

《Stop Rogue AI Act》：要求把每个agent登记在册

众议员Gottheimer与Lawler提案，要求NIST一年内出台agent部署标准：持续可机读清单、动作核验、防篡改日志。大部分机构自愿，但竞标新联邦合同的承包商得遵守。背景是Hugging Face等agent事件。

来源Rep. Mike Lawler · 9月3日

05

Anthropic在马萨诸塞AI法案上和同行分道扬镳

马州提案要求每四个月做一次独立灾难性风险评估。Anthropic和安全组织支持；OpenAI与Google反对，更偏伊利诺伊那种年度审计，并担心各州规则碎片化。

来源PYMNTS · 9月3日
