GPT-6 Astra can drive your screen

OpenAI dropped GPT-6 Astra and a full-duplex voice API in the same window — while California locked down companion chatbots for kids.

01

GPT-6 Astra ships for coding and computer use

OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra for computer use, coding, professional workflows, and cybersecurity — the first OpenAI model rated at the “critical” cyber capability level under its Preparedness Framework. It’s rolling out to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise, plus the API, Azure, and Bedrock.

SourcesInfoQ, Sep 10

02

GPT-Live-1 hits the API

OpenAI opened GPT-Live-1 for developers: full-duplex voice that listens while it speaks, can hand hard work to backends like Astra, and costs $0.05/min for the front-end voice layer.

SourcesOpenAI, Sep 10

03

ChatGPT for Financial Services

A finance-specific ChatGPT Work product pairs Astra with premium data (Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News) and bank-style governance. OpenAI shaped it with feedback from Morgan Stanley and Evercore.

SourcesVentureBeat, Sep 10

04

Cohere opens North Small Translate

Open-weight 218B MoE translation model (25B active) covering 50+ languages. Cohere reports 83.6 on WMT26 across all languages in its eval, ahead of DeepL NextGen and Google Translate.

SourcesCohere, Sep 10

05

California signs chatbot and kids’ social laws

Newsom signed “Adam’s Law” for companion chatbot safety (parental controls, crisis protocols, independent child-safety audits), banned addictive social feeds for under-16s, and put a four-year ban on AI chatbot toys.

SourcesCalifornia Governor’s Office, Sep 10

会点屏幕的模型来了，加州也给陪伴聊天机器人上了紧箍咒

今天真正要紧的事：OpenAI 同一窗口推了 GPT-6 Astra 和全双工语音 API，加州同步给陪伴型聊天机器人套上儿童安全紧箍咒。

01

GPT-6 Astra：会用电脑、会写代码

OpenAI 发布 GPT-6 Astra，主打电脑操控、编程和专业工作流；也是其 Preparedness Framework 下首个「关键」网络安全能力等级模型。正陆续对 ChatGPT Plus / Pro / Business / Enterprise、API、Azure 和 Bedrock 开放。

来源InfoQ，9月10日

02

GPT-Live-1 正式进 API

开发者可用上全双工语音模型：边听边说、可把重活甩给 Astra 等后端，前端语音层标价 $0.05/分钟。

来源OpenAI，9月10日

03

ChatGPT 金融版上线

面向投行与研究的 ChatGPT Work 行业版，把 Astra 和 Daloopa、PitchBook、LSEG News 等数据绑在一起，并带上企业治理控件；成型过程有摩根士丹利、Evercore 反馈。

来源VentureBeat，9月10日

04

Cohere 开源翻译模型 North Small Translate

218B MoE（激活 25B）开放权重翻译模型，覆盖 50+ 语言。Cohere 称在 WMT26 全语言评测拿到 83.6，高于自家对比里的 DeepL NextGen 和 Google Translate。

来源Cohere，9月10日

05

加州签署聊天机器人与儿童社媒新法

纽森签署「Adam’s Law」：陪伴型聊天机器人要有家长控制、危机协议和独立儿童安全审计；同时禁止向 16 岁以下提供成瘾式信息流，并对 AI 聊天玩具设四年禁售。

来源加州州长办公室，9月10日
