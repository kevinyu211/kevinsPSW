# Credits

Every model in `buildings.glb` and `props.glb` was made for this site with Python scripts run in
Blender 5.3 (`tools/city3d/island/`). No third-party models, textures or images are used, and nothing
was made with AI image or 3D generation.

- Lettering on the signs is Blender's built-in font (Bfont, based on DejaVu Sans, a free font),
  turned into flat geometry.
- Compression: meshoptimizer (MIT) through glTF Transform (MIT). Both are build tools only.

If a third-party model is ever added, it must be CC0 and must be listed here with its source and licence.
